# xnote app icon

## Design

Use a pure white rounded tile with a softly rounded notebook and stylus. Keep
the accepted lake blue hue, lightened another 20% toward white, with #88C3EA as
the central color and a gentle gradient from #A3D5F5 at the upper left to #7AB3E0 at the
lower right. White cover strokes
and a small writing curve give the notebook a relaxed expression.

Keep the approved blue draft's geometry, tilt, spacing and white background
unchanged. Apply the same blue gradient to the notebook and stylus.

## Clarity refinement (2026-11-04)

The small Finder icon looked soft even though all ICNS sizes were present.
Keep the approved gradient stops unchanged. Scale the symbol to 96% (about 10%
smaller than the first clarity preview), strengthen
the white strokes, and add a narrow blue contour to separate the pale fill from
the white background. Render each ICNS size directly from the vector instead of
downsampling the 1024px PNG. Use `build/icon.icns` explicitly for macOS packaging.

The build and exact per-frame vector-render checks passed. The packaged ICNS
matches `build/icon.icns`. The current default packages are
`release/xnote-1.0.0-arm64.dmg` and `release/xnote-1.0.0-arm64-mac.zip`.
Both installers were checked against the latest ICNS; the DMG was mounted
read-only to verify its actual app contents. The sidebar and welcome screen
also use `public/favicon.svg` for consistent branding.

## Assets

- `public/favicon.svg`: editable vector source and browser favicon.
- `build/icon.png`: 1024 × 1024 desktop icon, rasterized from the SVG.
- `build/icon.icns`: macOS icon with directly rendered size representations.
- `docs/app-icon.png`: 256px preview.
- `docs/app-icon-gradient.svg`: final gradient design.
- `docs/app-icon-gradient.png`: 384px preview.

The rounded tile has transparent outer corners. The tile itself is pure white;
the symbol uses blue shades and white, without material textures or shadows.
